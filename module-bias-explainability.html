Module 4

GenAI explainability and algorithmic bias

Explore bias sources, explainability limits, fairness checks, governance, and patient communication.

AdvancedBias, fairness, explainability, communication, governanceFairness, transparency, equity, patient communication

Learning outcomes

  • Identify sources of bias in data, prompts, model design, deployment, and user interpretation.
  • Explain why GenAI explanations need verification.
  • Apply fairness and transparency checks before using AI outputs in education or practice.

Teaching activities

  • Review a biased AI-generated patient explanation.
  • Create a fairness checklist for a GenAI healthcare use case.
  • Discuss how to explain AI limitations to patients and families.

Assessment

Identify bias risks in a case and propose mitigation steps.
