Module 2

GenAI prompting and healthcare use

Practice safer prompting for health education while protecting privacy and avoiding clinical overreach.

AppliedPrompt structure, healthcare context, constraints, boundaries, safe tasksPatient confidentiality, consent, professional responsibility

Learning outcomes

  • Write prompts with role, task, context, constraints, and output format.
  • Separate low-risk educational uses from high-risk clinical decision uses.
  • Apply privacy-safe prompting rules when handling patient-related scenarios.

Teaching activities

  • Rewrite a vague prompt into a structured healthcare prompt.
  • Classify example GenAI uses by risk.
  • Create a prompt checklist for students or clinicians.

Assessment

Improve a weak prompt and explain what risk was reduced.

Ethics focus

Patient confidentiality, consent, professional responsibility
