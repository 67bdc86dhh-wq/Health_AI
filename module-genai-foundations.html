Module 1

GenAI & LLMs: how they work

Understand how large language models generate outputs, where hallucinations come from, and why human oversight matters in healthcare.

FoundationModel logic, tokens, prompts, training, outputs, hallucination, limitationsHuman oversight, privacy, transparency

Learning outcomes

  • Explain GenAI, LLMs, tokens, prompts, model training, and generated outputs.
  • Identify common healthcare education uses such as summarisation, tutoring, drafting, and simulation.
  • Recognise hallucination, outdated knowledge, missing clinical context, and overconfident wording.

Teaching activities

  • Map one healthcare education task into a GenAI workflow.
  • Compare a safe model response with an unsafe response.
  • Mark where human review is required before use.

Assessment

Explain to a colleague why a fluent LLM answer is not clinical evidence.
